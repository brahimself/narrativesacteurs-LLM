\chapter*{Resume}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Resume}
Ce livrable 2 UE Chef d'oeuvre presente le resultat complet du projet:
donnees, pipeline, experimentations, evaluations et demonstrateur.
Le pipeline retenu pour l'evaluation finale combine extraction d'evenements,
traduction FR->EN des timelines francaises (fichiers \texttt{fr\_en}),
construction de paires cross-lingues \texttt{en <-> fr\_en},
fine-tuning d'encodeurs de similarite, puis generation controlee par seuil.
Les experimentations couvrent 248 entites dans le dataset d'apprentissage
final et comparent une baseline non fine-tunee a deux modeles fine-tunes
(\textit{allMiniLM} et \textit{MPNET}).
Une branche \texttt{merge/final\_en} a aussi ete implementee comme
experimentation complementaire, mais n'est pas la base des scores finaux
reportes dans ce document.

\vspace{0.5cm}
\noindent\textbf{Mots-cles:} LLM, sentence-encoder, similarite semantique, chronologies, traduction FR->EN, Hugging Face Spaces.

\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}
This Livrable 2 report (UE Chef d'oeuvre) presents the full project output:
data, pipeline, experiments, evaluation, and demo application.
The final evaluation pipeline combines timeline extraction,
FR->EN translation of French timelines (\texttt{fr\_en} files),
cross-lingual pair construction (\texttt{en <-> fr\_en}),
similarity encoder fine-tuning, and threshold-based controlled generation.
Experiments use 248 entities in the final training/evaluation dataset and
compare one non-fine-tuned baseline with two fine-tuned models
(\textit{allMiniLM} and \textit{MPNET}).
A \texttt{merge/final\_en} branch was also implemented as a complementary
experiment, but it is not the basis of the final similarity scores reported.
